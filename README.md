## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WUlplcMpOCEmTGBtBW/giphy.gif" width="28" /> About Me

<img align="right" width="330" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Anmol-Baranwal/gifs@main/coding.gif" alt="coding" />

I'm a **Computer Engineering undergrad at Pune Institute of Computer Technology** who spends most of his time at the seam between AI research and shipped software — the part where a clever model has to survive latency budgets, CI pipelines, and real users.

- 🔭 Currently building **VisionAI** — a distributed ML observability platform with a causal fairness engine and an agentic proxy that gates deployments
- 🧠 Deep in **LLM orchestration** with LangChain, LangGraph, Gemini and Vertex AI — agentic systems, not chatbots
- 💼 Previously interned at **Accenture** (Java Spring Boot, React) and **Cisco** (Python network automation over NETCONF/RESTCONF)
- 🏆 **1st Prize — Mastercard Code for Change**, **Winner — Inspiron 5.0**, **Top 10 — Google Solutions Challenge**
- 📝 Published two papers: *Impairment Assistance Software* (IRJMETS) and *Crop Yield Forecast Using ML* (IJASTR)
- 🎓 **Amazon ML Summer School '26** · CS50x / CS50P / CS50 SQL — Harvard
- 🌱 Learning more about distributed systems, inference optimization, and everything that makes ML boring and reliable
- 💬 Ask me about **agentic architectures, GCP Cloud Run, or shipping a full-stack app that handles real money**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I entered engineering through a lateral diploma route and finished the diploma at 96.11% — the scenic path works
